## Scope

Landing page (`src/pages/index.astro`) plus the shared design tokens and
components it uses, which the docs surface inherits. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

## Audience & job

Platform / SRE leads and reviewers evaluating whether to adopt an independent
authorisation record for their agents. They must, in one viewport, grasp the
mechanism (agent stops → named human decides → agent resumes on a signed
receipt) and either run the quickstart or become a design partner. Proof, not
claims; no invented customers, logos, benchmarks, or prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Fleetwrit is the office of record for machine actions — a signed,
tamper-evident register of who authorised exactly what. It refuses the category
default (the bright dev-tool SaaS page: sky gradient, pastel card kit,
eyebrow-over-heading, pill-everything) and its neon-terminal opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Cool bond-paper ground (#F7F8F5 / alt #ECEEEA), ink near-black text
(#15171C). One ultramarine ink accent (#2B27A6) carries actions, links and the
seal; two stamp inks — record-red (#B3261E), verify-green (#1C6B49) — appear
only on struck marks. Hairline rules (1px #D7D9D0), near-square 4px corners, no
pillow shadows; depth is a single crisp fine shadow on the one hero instrument.
Type: Archivo (institutional grotesque) for display and the wordmark; Public
Sans for body/UI; Spline Sans Mono for all registered data — sequence numbers,
timestamps, ids, fingerprints, statuses — set in tabular figures. Signature: the
struck register entry and the wax-free engraved seal; fingerprints and receipts
are first-class typographic material, never decoration.

STORY: A platform lead sees an agent action stopped, routed to a named reviewer
drawn from their own directory, decided, and released on a signed receipt — the
whole mechanism proven in the first viewport. They conclude this is the one
authoritative place recording who authorised what across their agents, and run
the quickstart or apply to the design-partner programme.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two columns on a bond-paper ground, a hairline rule down the
gutter. Left (~52%): an institutional headline in Archivo at architectural
scale, tight tracking, no eyebrow; a one-sentence deck; primary action "Run the
quickstart" (solid ultramarine, 4px corners) and secondary "Become a design
partner" (hairline). Right: the hero instrument — a ruled "Certificate of
authorisation" slip with a fine shadow: the request (roll back payments-api),
the routed line (Okta group platform-sre → queue sre-oncall, 4 reviewers), the
decision with a struck APPROVED seal in ultramarine, and a signed-receipt line
carrying a real fingerprint hash and a Verify control.

FORM: Records office / chain-of-custody register — grounded candidate #5, the
roll's assignment; seed key 76517f0d. Raised by donations: restraint from the
civic-bureau challenger (colour spent on one element, two weights of one face);
scale-contrast + one live interaction from the variable-font specimen; state as
a struck mark from the cutting-bench; measured before/after tabular figures from
the forge.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
